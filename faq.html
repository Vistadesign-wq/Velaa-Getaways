What is Velaa Getaways?
Velaa Getaways is a Maldives-based travel company offering personalised holiday experiences, local island accommodation, excursions and travel assistance.
What services do you offer?
We arrange:
  • Local island guesthouse stays
  • Customised holiday packages
  • Airport transfers
  • Excursions and marine adventures
  • Professional photoshoots
  • Romantic beach and floating dinners
How can I book a holiday?
Send us an enquiry through the form on our Plan your trip page, or message us on WhatsApp. Share your travel dates, number of guests and preferred package, and we’ll prepare a personalised quotation.
Can I customise my holiday package?
Absolutely! We can tailor your holiday to your budget, preferred accommodation, activities and travel dates.
Do you arrange airport transfers?
Yes! We arrange speedboat transfers between Velana International Airport and selected local islands, subject to availability. Transfers are included in our packages.
What payment methods do you accept?
Available payment methods may include bank transfer, card payment and cash. Accepted options will be confirmed before booking.
Is an advance payment required?
Yes. A $200 deposit confirms your booking, and the balance is paid upon arrival. Exact amounts and deadlines are confirmed in writing before you book.
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellation and refund conditions vary depending on the accommodation and services booked. The applicable terms are provided before we confirm your reservation.
Can I book excursions separately?
Yes! You can book individual excursions without purchasing a complete holiday package, subject to availability.
What happens if the weather is bad?
Your safety is our priority. Excursions may be rescheduled or cancelled due to unfavourable weather or sea conditions. Alternative arrangements or refunds depend on the applicable booking terms.
Is the Maldives suitable for budget travellers?
Definitely! Local islands offer affordable accommodation, beautiful beaches, exciting excursions and authentic Maldivian experiences.
Can you arrange honeymoon or romantic experiences?
Yes! We can arrange romantic dinners, private experiences, professional photoshoots and special celebrations.
Do I need a visa to visit the Maldives?
Tourist visas are generally available on arrival for eligible visitors, subject to immigration requirements. Please check the latest official entry requirements before travelling.
What currency is accepted on the island?
The official currency is the Maldivian Rufiyaa (MVR). US dollars are also widely accepted by tourism businesses.
