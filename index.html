03Why Velaa

Travel with people who call this home

01

Insight & curiosityTwo perspectives, one welcome

Travel with the insight of someone who calls Thoddoo home and the curiosity of someone who once arrived here as a visitor. We bring both perspectives into the way we plan and host our trips.

02

Family-run hostsCloser to local life

We work with local hosts and family-run guesthouses. You’ll have the chance to experience the island through the people, places and everyday rhythms that make Thoddoo what it is.

03

Reef, palms & white sandNature all around

On Thoddoo, nature is all around you and there’s no need to rush past it. Snorkel over the reef and look out for turtles, wander beneath the palms, or settle on the white sand and listen to the waves. The island’s gentle rhythm gives you space to breathe and be present.

04

Planned around youExperiences that feel personal

Choose the kind of island days you want: time on the water, a nearby island visit, a beach dinner or a slower stay. We’ll help you explore the options and plan around what matters to you.

05

Clear & transparentLocal stays, considered prices

Our packages are built around locally run guesthouses and island experiences, with options for different travel styles and budgets. We make the price and inclusions clear before you book.
