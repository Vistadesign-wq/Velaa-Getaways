Welcome to Thoddoo

A real Maldivian island where turtles glide past the reef, watermelons ripen in the fields, coconuts hang from the palms — and you are welcomed like a friend.

Thoddoo is a small, green, real Maldivian island — farms and palm roads in the middle, white sand and coral reef all around. Here is what makes a week here special.
